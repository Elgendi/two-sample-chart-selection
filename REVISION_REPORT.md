# Revision 5.0 — preferred visual style with stronger computational evidence

The manuscript keeps its original title, article typography, worked-score figure, eight familiar-chart examples and family-by-task overview. The eight display-transfer examples remain in the supplement. Three additional simple figures show the complete strong-default comparison, decoder dependence and synthetic transfer outcomes.

The scientific evidence was recovered from the existing version 4.1 reproducibility archive, not invented during rewriting. The accompanying run report documents the fresh rebuild. The main manuscript now distinguishes 530 empirical task instances from independent source units; incorporates resource/patient-held-out defaults; retains 228 synthetic task instances from 156 inputs; and includes a separately implemented geometry decoder. No new human or clinical data are claimed.
