# Guide to the revised figures

1. **How the score works:** WDBC mean area, raw dots 97.70 versus mean bars 60.17. The score calculation and full seven-family optimized comparison are shown. This uses the historical 21-setting search.
2. **Eight different selected families:** eight uniquely winning families across five questions, one fixed configuration per eligible family. Box plots win the illustrated spread task (98.65 versus 53.74); the pie example scores 99.950 versus 99.725 for bars. Each panel names the question and candidate count.
3. **Does the choice survive a new display size?** Eight distinct reference-selected families; seven lose to another family at the new size and one pie selection remains optimal. Both displayed images are at 384 pixels. Examples maximize the deficit for each selected-family/task combination and are intentionally diagnostic.
4. **Question first, chart second:** all 13 families across six questions. Values include exact tied winning memberships. Zero means evaluated without a win; a dash means no implemented contract.

`task_example_values.csv` (also `figure_values.csv`) maps the 16 example panels and full-precision scores. `scoring_example_values.csv` contains Figure 1 values. `task_figure_checks.json` records selection and display checks. `figures/panels` provides 16 enlarged individual cards; `Expanded_example_gallery.pdf` combines them with Figure 1. SVG, PDF and PNG versions are included.

These are enlarged scored raster images with publication labels; they are not generative illustrations. Scores come from four original-size placements and cannot be interpreted as human-performance percentages. The main figure examples are selected retrospectively. All candidates, failures, ties and transfer outcomes are available in the complete code archive.
