# Reader-study preparation — no human observations

The authoritative six-task protocol is `../READER_STUDY_PROTOCOL.md`. It specifies a future experiment; no approval, recruitment, sample size or outcome is claimed. Complete development, pilot and untouched final-test manifests before registration. Keep truth and identifiers out of participant-facing materials.

The inherited `response_template.csv` is a legacy five-gap response format only, not a complete six-task collection instrument. `planning.py` and its CSV output are hypothetical planning sensitivities, not a completed power calculation for the final crossed participant/case design. Do not treat their outputs as human evidence.

For each final task, record case and participant pseudonyms, assigned condition, stimulus checksum, numerical response(s), units, completion time, confidence, missingness reason and display metadata. Freeze scoring, invalid-response rules and analysis before collection. Do not substitute zeros for missing responses.
